About the Club

A stage built for
sharper thinking.

The Debate & Dialogue Club at AIKTC is a gathering place for students who believe the strongest ideas are the ones tested in conversation. Through structured debate, open dialogue, and rigorous argument, we prepare our members to speak with clarity, think with precision, and lead with conviction.
